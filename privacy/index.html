Privacy Policy

Who We Are

This privacy policy covers Brain Race Think and Drive, which is operated by Hubert Kiełkowski, together with the related website or services described here. It covers the app on iOS.

What Data We Collect

Information users provide directly may include:

  • Account and sign-in details needed to create or manage a user account
  • Purchase or subscription information used to unlock or manage paid features
  • Game progress
  • best scores
  • badges
  • per-category statistics and app settings (language
  • music
  • sound effects
  • haptics) — stored locally on the device

Information collected automatically through the app or related services may include:

  • Identifiers needed to recognize an account, device, session, or subscription state

Some data leaves the device to support app functionality such as accounts, syncing, purchases, support, or other online features.

How We Use Data

We use your data in a variety of ways, including:

  • To manage billing-related access and entitlements.

The following providers are used:

  • Payments: Apple StoreKit (App Store) and RevenueCat. Used for processing a single one-time in-app purchase, validating the App Store receipt and unlocking the full version.
  • Website Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub and Inc.). Used for hosting the marketing website.

Additional feature details:

  • StoreKit: A single one-time purchase (non-consumable) that unlocks the full version permanently. There is no subscription and no recurring charge. The purchase can be restored at any time from Settings using the same Apple Account.

When Data is Shared

When an app feature needs online processing, syncing, account handling, or support, relevant data may be sent off the device to operate that feature.

Some data may be shared with outside recipients such as RevenueCat and Inc. (USA) — purchase validation; Apple — App Store payment processing when that is needed to operate the app’s features.

The following providers are used:

  • Payments: Apple StoreKit (App Store) and RevenueCat. Used for processing a single one-time in-app purchase, validating the App Store receipt and unlocking the full version. May receive personal data needed for this role. Optional for users. Privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.
  • Website Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub and Inc.). Used for hosting the marketing website. May receive personal data needed for this role. Privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement.

Purchase data is shared with RevenueCat to validate the one-time purchase and unlock the full version across the user’s devices. RevenueCat identifies the installation by an anonymous ID that is not linked to a name, email address or account. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; the developer never receives payment or card details.

Payments and Subscriptions

The app offers in-app purchases or subscriptions through StoreKit.

Payment-related providers include Apple StoreKit (App Store) and RevenueCat. They are used for processing a single one-time in-app purchase, validating the App Store receipt and unlocking the full version. These providers may receive the purchase, billing, account, or receipt data needed to process payments and manage entitlements.

Purchase or subscription information may be used to manage billing, entitlements, support, refunds, or account access.

Data Retention

We keep data until the user deletes it, closes the account, or asks us to remove it, unless a longer retention period is legally required.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

Some permissions are optional and are only used if the user chooses the feature that needs them.

Users can ask for deletion of their data using the contact or tools described below.

Where supported, users can ask for a copy or export of their data.

Privacy requests can be sent through bashubb.dev@gmail.com.

Requests can be handled through Contact email, In-app deletion tools, and Manual support handling.

For users in the EEA or UK, applicable law may provide rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to request a copy of personal data.

For users in certain US states, including California, applicable law may provide rights to know, access, correct, delete, or opt out of certain processing of personal information, subject to legal exceptions.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed to children. The app is suitable for children but is not directed at them. It has no accounts, ads, tracking or analytics, and player data stays on the device. Parents can contact bashubb.dev@gmail.com.

International Transfers

Purchase data is processed by RevenueCat, Inc. in the United States, and the website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. Transfers outside the EEA rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Third-Party Websites and Services

The following providers are used:

  • Website Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub and Inc.). Used for hosting the marketing website. May receive personal data needed for this role. Privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement.

The website is a static marketing page with no forms, no cookies and no analytics. It links to the App Store listing.
